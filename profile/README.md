**Your AI App. Served.**

Cloche is a home for the apps people make with AI agents. Build one in Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or Cursor, put it online from the chat, and share it with a link. The people you send it to open the app. Nothing else.

**Connect your agent.** Add Cloche as a connector at `https://mcp.cloche.dev/mcp`, or give your agent this line:

> Fetch `https://cloche.dev/agent-setup/prompt.md` - it has everything you need to get started.

Here you will find the pieces that live next to your agent: the [Cursor and Grok Bot plugin](https://github.com/cloche-it/cursor-plugin).
